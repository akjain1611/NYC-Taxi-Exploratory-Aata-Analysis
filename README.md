**NYC Taxi Operations & Demand Analysis**

**Project Overview**

This project performs large-scale Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on NYC Yellow Taxi trip data to uncover:

1. Demand patterns
2. Revenue trends
3. Traffic congestion insights
4. Passenger behavior
5. Zone-wise taxi activity
6. Pricing and tipping patterns

The analysis was performed using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and GeoPandas on sampled NYC taxi datasets.

**Key Insights**

1. Peak taxi demand occurs during: 7–9 AM and 4–7 PM
2. Manhattan and JFK Airport dominate taxi activity.
3. Credit cards account for over 81% of payments.
4. Revenue peaks during: Spring (Q2) and Fall (Q4)
5. Fare amount strongly correlates with trip distance.
6. Weekend travel shows higher group passenger behavior.
